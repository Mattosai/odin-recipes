General Tso's Ground Turkey

Description

A simple, flavorful dinner that comes together in under 20 minutes. Swap the kale for a different leafy green, or omit completely and add whatever veggies speak to you. Serve this turkey with rice, or your favorite rice substitute. It's sure to be a hit every time.

Ingredients

  • 2lb Ground Turkey
  • 1 Jar Trader Joe's General Tso Sauce
  • 8oz Kale
  • 2 Tbsp Oil

Steps:

  1. Add oil to a large saute or skillet over medium heat. Add kale and cook until it begins to wilt, 4-5 minutes.
  2. Add turkey and use spatula or other cooking utisil to break it up into small crumbles. Fully cook turkey until it's completely browned, about 10-15 minutes.
  3. Once turkey is finished cooking, remove it from heat and add general tso sauce. Mix until it is well incorporated, and serve.
