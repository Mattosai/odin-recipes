Chicken Shawarma

Description

You can still get that delicious flavor of chicken shawarma without the need for a verticle rotisserie! This recipe uses a spice blend that will remind you of a doner kebab, but is cooked in a pan, or on a grill. Enjoy the chicken with a side of salad and veggies, or eat it in a pita with your favorite accoutrements.

Ingredients

  • 2lb Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs

Marinade

  • 1-2 Garlic Clove(s)
  • 1 Tbsp Ground Coriander
  • 1 Tbsp Ground Cumin
  • 1 Tbsp Group Cardamom
  • 1 Tsp Cayenne Pepper
  • 2 Tsp Smoked Paprika
  • 2 Tsp Salt
  • 2 Tbsp Lemon Juice
  • 3 Tbsp Olive Oil
  • Black Pepper (to taste)

Steps:

  1. Start by combining all marinade ingredients. Then in a bag, or container, place all the chicken and pour in the marinade. Make sure to coat the chicken completely, and leave it to marinate for at least two hours, ideally overnight. Don't marinate too long as the lemon juice can start to denature the chicken and give it an unpleasant texture.
  2. Heat 1 Tbsp olive oil in a large skillet on medium high heat, or brush olive oil over grill grates and heat to medium high heat.
  3. Place chicken in pan or on grill, cooking the first side for 4-5 minutes. Once a nice char has formed, turn chicken and cook for an additional 3-4 minutes. If using a pan you may need to cook in batches. Add additional oil as needed each batch.
  4. The chicken is done when it has reached an internal temperature of 165 degrees ferenheit. Remove chicken from grill or pan, and cover loosely with foil. Let it rest, covered, for at least 5 minutes.
