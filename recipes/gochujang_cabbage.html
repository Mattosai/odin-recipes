Gochujang Cabbage

  • 1 Head of cabbage
  • 2 Tbsp Unsalted Butter
  • 1 Tbsp Gochujang
  • 1 Tbsp Oil

Steps:

  1. Cut cabbage in half, and then cut each half into 3-4 wedges. Make sure to cut through the stem to make sure each wedge stays together
  2. In a large frying pan, sear each side of the cabbage wedges on medium-high heat until surface takes on some charring, about 3-4 minutes per side
  3. Once the cabbage is seared, pour 1/4 to 1/2 cup water into the pan and cover with a lid. Steam until all the water has evaporated
  4. While the cabbage is steaming, mix gochujang and butter until well combined. You can use less or more gochujang depending on spice preference
  5. After the cabbage is done steaming, remove the pan from heat. Add gochujang butter and use residual heat to melt the mixture, saucing all sides of the cabbage
